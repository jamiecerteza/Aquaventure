What is Aquaventure Fitness?

We have two branches conveniently located to serve you better. Aquaventure Fitness Gym (AFG) is a fitness and aquatic center providing strength training, physical conditioning, and competitive swim programs across the Rizal province. Its main fitness facility is located at Angono, Rizal and Binangonan, Rizal; while its associated aquatic and swim training programs operate at the same location as its branch in Binangonan, Rizal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the gym rates?+

Rates shown on this page include ₱80 per session for members, ₱100 per session for non-members, and ₱900 per month for non-members. Please confirm current prices with the branch.

What membership packages are listed?+

The page lists Basic (1 month), Silver (2 months), Gold (4 months), Platinum (6 months), and Palladium (1 year) packages. Confirm current package details with the branch.
